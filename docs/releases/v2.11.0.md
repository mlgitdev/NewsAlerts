## 🚀 Overview

NewsAlerts **v2.11.0** introduces native integration with **BamSEC (by AlphaSense)**, providing day traders with instant, real-time access to official SEC EDGAR filings directly alongside catalyst wire news. Traders can now spot offerings, dilution risks, shelf registrations (S-1/S-3/F-3/424B), insider accumulation (Form 3/4/13D), and material press releases (8-K/6-K) without leaving the terminal.

---

## 🌟 Key New Features & Capabilities

### 1. Real-Time BamSEC SEC Filings Integration (Issue #57)
- **Automatic Scraper & Caching Service (`app/bamsec.py`)**:
  - Live fetch and structured extraction of US equity filings directly from BamSEC with in-memory TTL caching (120s) and multi-threaded safety.
  - Automatically parses filing categories:
    - **News (6-K / 8-K)**: Press releases, dividend declarations, asset sales.
    - **Offerings & Dilution (S-1, S-3, F-3, 424B, DRS, RW)**: Secondary offerings, warrant issuances, shelf registrations.
    - **Ownership & Insiders (Form 3, Form 4, SC 13D, SC 13G)**: Major activist stakes and insider purchases.
    - **Financials (10-K, 10-Q, 20-F)**: Quarterly and annual earnings reports.
    - **Proxies & Other**: Proxy statements (DEF 14A) and regulatory communications.

### 2. Dual Display Modes: Collapsible Drawer & Sub-Tabs
- **⚡ Collapsible Accordion Drawer (Top of Wire News)**:
  - Collapsible card at the top of the news panel showing filing totals and quick summary counts (`News`, `Dilution`, `Insiders`).
  - Expanding the drawer reveals category filter pills and filing rows with color-coded institutional badges.
  - State is saved persistently in `App.config.news.bamsec_drawer_open`.
- **📑 Sub-Tabs View (`📰 Wire News` / `📑 BamSEC Filings`)**:
  - Dedicated sub-tab bar under the ticker inspector.
  - Full-featured BamSEC explorer with real-time keyword search, category filters, and grouped filing cards.
  - Clicking any symbol from the screener or tape defaults back to the `📰 Wire News` tab for immediate catalyst analysis.
- **On-The-Fly Layout Toggle**:
  - Click `⊞ Tabs View` or `⊟ Drawer View` right in the news bar to toggle between layouts instantly.
  - Configurable default mode in **Settings &rarr; General**.

### 3. One-Click BamSEC Launching & Direct Filing Links
- **Header Action Button (`[📑 BamSEC ↗]`)**: Docked next to the TradingView chart button to jump directly to the company's full directory on BamSEC.com.
- **Inline Filing Links**: Clicking any filing row opens the document view on BamSEC in the browser.
- **Filing Count Badge (`📑 17 SEC`)**: Interactive badge next to the borrow tag to open filings immediately.

### 4. Chrome Full-Screen (F11) Showcase & Privacy Protocol
- Documented in `AGENTS.md` Section 6: native Chrome full-screen (`F11`) uncropped documentation captures, mandatory credential scrambling, and pitch brochure hero showcases.
