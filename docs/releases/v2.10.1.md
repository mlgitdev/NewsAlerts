# NewsAlerts v2.10.1 — Release Notes

**Release Date:** October 2026  
**Type:** Patch Release (`PATCH` — Momentum Tape Squeeze Filter Precision & 5-Min Watchlist Auto-Sync)  
**Single Source of Truth:** `VERSION` (`2.10.1`)

---

## 🚀 Overview

NewsAlerts **v2.10.1** resolves critical false-positive surge triggers in the **Running Momentum Tape Engine** and introduces **5-Minute Periodic Auto-Refresh Synchronization** for TradingView Cloud Watchlists.

In previous versions, micro-cap stocks with high intraday percentage changes (such as `STAK`) could repeatedly trigger `Squeeze Alert - Up 5% in 5min` and `Up 10% in 10min` on the tape even while consolidating tightly or declining from premarket highs. Version 2.10.1 establishes rigorous rolling-window upward progression rules, eliminates invalid premarket server-velocity fallbacks, and ensures watchlists auto-synchronize symbol counts without manual button clicks.

---

## 🛠️ Key Bug Fixes & Enhancements

### 1. Elimination of False Squeeze Alerts on Flat & Declining Stocks (Issue #46)
- **Pre/Post-Market Server-Velocity Isolation**:
  - Identified that TradingView screener `change|5` and `change|15` fields return static deltas against yesterday's regular session close during extended hours (e.g. `STAK` returned ~9.68% and ~11.43%).
  - Restricted TradingView `change|5` and `change|15` velocity evaluation strictly to regular market sessions (`session == "reg"`). In premarket and postmarket sessions, surge velocity is now exclusively calculated from verified in-memory tick-by-tick rolling history (`st.hist`).
- **Strict Rolling-Window Surge Geometry**:
  - Enforces upward chronological validation: the window low must occur *before* the surge high (`t_min < t_max`). Downward moves or selloffs from peaks can never trigger a squeeze alert.
  - Enforces crest proximity: current price must be actively near the surge peak (`price >= max_p * 0.975`). Stocks that dumped from peaks (e.g. `STAK` down -13% from $2.13) are strictly rejected.
- **Anti-Spam Price Progression Rule**:
  - Once alerted at price $P_{alert}$, the tape will **never** re-fire at the same or lower price.
  - Ongoing surge alerts require a progressive price advancement of at least $+1.0\%$ above the previous alert price ($P_{new} \ge P_{alert} \times 1.01$).
  - Once the time window has elapsed, re-alerting requires a completely new local low formed after the previous alert followed by a fresh $+up\_pct\%$ surge.
- **Removed Flawed Intraday Fallback**:
  - Eliminated the fallback `not st.hist and session_chg >= up_pct` which previously treated any stock up $+5\%$ or $+10\%$ on the day as having surged in 5 minutes.

### 2. TradingView Watchlist 5-Minute Auto-Refresh Synchronization (Issue #46)
- Integrated an automatic background synchronization interval (`setInterval`) running every **5 minutes** (300,000 ms) in `initWatchlistControl()`.
- Automatically queries TradingView Cloud Watchlists (`/api/tv_watchlists`), refreshing symbol counts and titles (e.g., updating when stocks are removed externally in TradingView).
- Preserves the currently selected watchlist in the dropdown without disruption or jumping.
- Background sync runs silently without disruptive toasts; manual clicks on the `🔄` button continue to provide visual spinning feedback and confirmation toasts.
